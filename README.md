## Business Problem

Marketing teams invest significant budgets across multiple campaign types and regions, but often lack clear visibility into which campaigns generate the highest return on investment.

Without a centralized performance view, businesses may continue investing in low-performing campaigns while missing opportunities in high-performing regions and marketing channels.

This dashboard helps identify:

* Which campaigns generate the highest ROI
* Which regions deliver the strongest returns
* How marketing spend compares to generated revenue
* Where future marketing budgets should be allocated for maximum impact

## Project Goal

The objective of this dashboard is to evaluate marketing campaign effectiveness by analyzing revenue, spend, ROI, campaign performance, and regional trends. The dashboard supports data-driven decisions for budget allocation and campaign optimization.

## Dashboard Features

- KPI Cards for Revenue, Spend, ROI, and Best Campaign
- Stacked Column Chart for Revenue vs Spend Analysis
- Campaign ROI Comparison
- Regional Performance Analysis
- Interactive Slicers
- Gauge Chart for ROI Tracking

## Skills Demonstrated

- Power BI

- Data Modeling

## Key Insights

- Influencer Marketing emerged as the highest-performing campaign based on ROI.
- Total Revenue substantially exceeded Total Spend, indicating positive overall campaign performance.
- Regional performance varied significantly, suggesting opportunities for targeted budget allocation.
- Digital marketing channels generated stronger returns compared to traditional campaign methods.
